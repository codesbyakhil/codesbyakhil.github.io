Shock dynamics · Compressible flows · HPC & GPU computing

Akhil S. Pillai

PhD Scholar, Department of Aerospace Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay.

I work on what happens when a shock wave meets something that can bend, break, or absorb it — how the wave loads a structure, and how the structure answers back into the flow. Before Bombay I was a Project Associate at IIT Madras, running direct numerical simulations of three-dimensional air–water flow past a circular cylinder with an in-house Fortran 90 solver: HLLC fluxes, MPI domain decomposition, OpenACC offload with one GPU per rank, and meshes of 8–30 million cells on PARAM Shakti and A100 nodes.
